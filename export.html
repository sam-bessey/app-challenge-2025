Export Drives

You have two options to export your drives:

- Driving form: This is the official form that you need to use to get your license. DriveTime can fill it out for you automatically, or you can fill it out yourself here if you really want to.

- CSV file: This is useful for backing up your drives or transferring them to DriveTime on another device. You can import drives from a CSV by clicking the "Import" button on the "My Drives" page, then selecting your CSV file.
