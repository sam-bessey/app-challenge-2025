About DriveTime

DriveTime is an app that lets people keep track of how much time they have of driving with their learner’s permit. In Maine, you need at least 70 hours, at least 10 of those at night, before you can get your license. DriveTime gives people a simple way to keep track of this. It lets you time your current drive, save a past drive that you might not have been able to save earlier, and view and edit your saved drives. It also keeps track of whether your drive was at night or not. After you finish your 70 hours, you can export your drives as a CSV file. This can be sent to another device, or can be imported into DriveTime later. You are also able to change your color theme in Settings.
