About DriveTime

DriveTime is designed to take the stress out of tracking your driving hours with your learner’s permit as you work toward your Maine driver’s license. Maine requires 70 total hours of driving, including at least 10 hours at night, before you can apply for a license. DriveTime gives people a simple way to keep track of this. DriveTime allows you to time your current drive, save a past drive that you might not have been able to save earlier, and view and edit your saved drives. It also keeps track of whether your drive was at day or night. Once your 70 hours are complete, you can export your data as a CSV file for easy backup or transfer, and you can even personalize the app with different color themes in Settings.
